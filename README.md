Vellas Global Nutrition Ltd
Commercializing Indigenous Nigerian Legumes for Food Security and Nutrition
Vellas Global Nutrition Ltd (CAC RC 9390805) is a NAFDAC-certified food technology and agro-processing company based at the Technology Incubation Centre (TIC), Independence Layout, Enugu, Enugu State, Nigeria.
We develop high-protein, composite flour baked goods and snack products using underutilized indigenous Nigerian legumes — including Bambara groundnut (Okpa), African black bean (Akidi), African yam bean (Uzaki), pigeon pea (Fiofio), and African breadfruit seed (Ukwa)
